Thank you. I have put everything in one place, in the form you asked for:

https://github.com/umbertomaravigna61-debug/unica/tree/main/contrib/yue2-fp8-cuda-graph

It contains the unified patch against the yue2_infer 0.1.5 wheel, the exact code/model revisions
(YuE2-3B 8f24312, YuE2-Vae 152733a, torch 2.10.0+cu128, full pip freeze), the reproduction commands,
the before/after logs, and the timings separated into load, plan, song (AR), NAR and decode, with peak
VRAM and peak RSS for each run. The eager baseline is in the same tables; the 4.5x figure is the
per-token composing time in the UNICA runner (0.131 s/token eager vs 0.0286 s/token with the graph),
the song stage alone measures 5.6x in the lab script, and a same-seed A/B run back to back today
gave 5.9x.

I have also opened the issue in the YuE repository: https://github.com/multimodal-art-projection/YuE/issues/262

Two honest notes. The graph path is not bit-identical to eager: in a same-seed A/B run back to back
(same lyrics, same NAR steps) the two token sequences diverge at the second token, while each path on
its own is deterministic (eager seed 8 gave exactly 3648 tokens on two different days). The A/B timings,
the token comparison, a signal analysis (loudness, true peak, crest factor, band energy, spectrograms,
chroma: technically equivalent) and my listening note (equal quality to my ear) are in the write-up. And all numbers come
from one machine and single runs — a data point, not a benchmark.

Two independent findings are in the same write-up: attention_backend="auto" selects FlashAttention on
the official Windows torch build where it is not compiled, and nar._offload_ar fails with FP8 weights
because FP8Linear has buffers rather than parameters.
